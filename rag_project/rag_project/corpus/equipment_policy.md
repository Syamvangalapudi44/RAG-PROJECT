EQUIPMENT AND ASSET POLICY
Effective 12 September 2022.

5.1 The company provides a laptop to every full-time employee.
5.2 Home office equipment shall not exceed INR 25,000 per employee
    in any rolling twelve-month period.
5.3 Contractors and vendor staff are not covered by this policy and
    must supply their own equipment.
5.4 Loss or damage must be reported within 48 hours.
