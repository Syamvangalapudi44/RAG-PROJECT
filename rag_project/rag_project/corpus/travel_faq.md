TRAVEL - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Who do I contact about travel?
A: The travel desk, at extension 4400.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: At least fourteen days where possible. Late bookings cost the
   company significantly more.

Q: Can I combine personal travel with a business trip?
A: Yes, provided the company pays no additional cost. Discuss the
   itinerary with your manager first.
