SEPARATION AND NOTICE

Notice period is determined by band, as follows.

| Band | Role level          | Notice period |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| 1    | Associate           | 30 days       |
| 2    | Engineer            | 30 days       |
| 3    | Senior Engineer     | 60 days       |
| 4    | Lead / Manager      | 90 days       |
| 5    | Director and above  | 90 days       |

Notice may be waived only by the reporting manager and HR jointly.
